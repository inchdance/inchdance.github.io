     <span style="color: rgb(255, 76, 0);">
      <strong>
       <span new="" style="line-height: 115%;" times="">
        What is improvisation?
       </span>
      </strong>
      <strong>
       <span style="line-height: 115%; font-family: SimSun; font-size: 16px;">
        什么是即兴戏剧？
       </span>
      </strong>
     </span>
    </p>
    <p style="font-family: 16px 0px">
     <span style="color: rgb(255, 76, 0);">
      <strong>
       <span style="line-height: 115%; font-family: SimSun; font-size: 16px;">
        <br/>
       </span>
      </strong>
     </span>
    </p>
    <p style="font-family: 16px 0px">
     <span style="line-height: 115%; text-decoration: none;">
      I
     </span>
     <span new="" style=" line-height: 115% " times="">
      mprovisation is the art of unscripted performance. In improv, there is nothing to memorize.
     </span>
    </p>
    <p style="font-family: 16px 0px">
     <span style=";line-height: 115%;font-family: SimSun;font-size: 16px">
      即兴戏剧是一种无剧本的表演艺术，因为无剧本，也就没有“背台词”一说。
     </span>
    </p>
    <p style="font-family: 16px 0px">
     <span style=";line-height: 115%;font-family: SimSun;font-size: 16px">
      <img data-ratio="0.665625" data-s="300,640" data-src="/assets/images/6c550c526a.jpeg" data-type="jpeg" data-w="1280" src="/assets/images/6c550c526a.jpeg" style="width: 100%; height: auto;"/>
     </span>
    </p>
    <p style="font-family: 16px 0px">
     <span new="" style=" line-height: 115% " times="">
      <br/>
     </span>
    </p>
    <p style="font-family: 16px 0px">
     <span new="" style=" line-height: 115% " times="">
      Improv is acting through reacting. Thus, it allows the actors to practice, experience and perform real-life situations in an infinite number of contexts.
     </span>
    </p>
    <p style="font-family: 16px 0px">
     <span style=";line-height: 115%;font-family: SimSun;font-size: 16px">
      演员在应对
     </span>
     <span new="" style=" line-height: 115% " times="">
      “
     </span>
     <span style=";line-height: 115%;font-family: SimSun;font-size: 16px">
      突发
     </span>
     <span new="" style=" line-height: 115% " times="">
      ”
     </span>
     <span style=";line-height: 115%;font-family: SimSun;font-size: 16px">
      场景时即兴进行表演创作，这样的方式使得演员可以在即兴表演过程中预演无数五花八门的生活场景。
     </span>
    </p>
    <p style="font-family: 16px 0px">
     <span style=";line-height: 115%;font-family: SimSun;font-size: 16px">
     </span>
    </p>
    <p>
     <br/>
    </p>
    <p style="font-family: 16px 0px">
     <span new="" style=" line-height: 115% " times="">
      Improv actors create a world that has characters, storylines, conflict, tensions, a beginning, middle and end.
     </span>
    </p>
    <p style="font-family: 16px 0px">
     <span style=";line-height: 115%;font-family: SimSun;font-size: 16px">
      演员能够即兴地创造出一个新的世界，这个世界里充满着不同的角色，故事，矛盾，戏剧张力，共同编织出新世界的开篇、高潮和结局。
     </span>
    </p>
    <p style="font-family: 16px 0px">
     <span style=";line-height: 115%;font-family: SimSun;font-size: 16px">
      <br/>
     </span>
    </p>
    <p style="font-family: 16px 0px">
     <span style=";line-height: 115%;font-family: SimSun;font-size: 16px">
     </span>
    </p>
    <p>
     <img data-ratio="0.665625" data-s="300,640" data-src="/assets/images/c7d70aa169.jpeg" data-type="jpeg" data-w="1280" src="/assets/images/c7d70aa169.jpeg" style="width: 100%; height: auto;"/>
    </p>
    <p style="font-family: 16px 0px">
     <span style=";line-height: 115%;font-family: SimSun;font-size: 16px">
     </span>
     <br/>
    </p>
    <p style="font-family: 16px 0px">
     <span new="" style=" line-height: 115% " times="">
      Because improv is so flexible, the actors can play with an endless variety of possibilities.
     </span>
    </p>
    <p style="font-family: 16px 0px">
     <span style=";line-height: 115%;font-family: SimSun;font-size: 16px">
      因为即兴表演不按套路出牌，演员可以玩味无穷无尽的可能性。
     </span>
    </p>
    <p style="font-family: 16px 0px">
     <span style=";line-height: 115%;font-family: SimSun;font-size: 16px">
      <br/>
     </span>
    </p>
    <p style="font-family: 16px 0px">
     <span new="" style=" line-height: 115% " times="">
      An improv performance may be funny or serious, long or short.
     </span>
    </p>
    <p style="font-family: 16px 0px">
     <span style=";line-height: 115%;font-family: SimSun;font-size: 16px">
      一场即兴演出可以很搞笑，也可以很严肃，或长篇，或短篇。
     </span>
    </p>
    <p style="font-family: 16px 0px">
     <span style=";line-height: 115%;font-family: SimSun;font-size: 16px">
      <br/>
     </span>
    </p>
    <p>
     <img data-ratio="0.665625" data-s="300,640" data-src="/assets/images/cb9a7c50bf.jpeg" data-type="jpeg" data-w="640" src="/assets/images/cb9a7c50bf.jpeg" style="width: 100%; height: auto;"/>
    </p>
    <p style="font-family: 16px 0px">

      <br/>

    </p>
    <p style="font-family: 16px 0px">
     <span new="" style=" line-height: 115% " times="">
      Improvisation is commonly used throughout the world to create movies, television shows, for brainstorming, corporate training and as a form of therapy.
     </span>
    </p>
    <p style="font-family: 16px 0px">
     <span style=";line-height: 115%;font-family: SimSun;font-size: 16px">
      这些有趣的特性使得即兴表演在全球范围内被广泛应用于电影、电视剧、电视节目的创作，是一种头脑风暴的方式；也被应用在企业培训、团队建设和心理治疗等领域。
     </span>
    </p>

    <p style="font-family: 16px 0px">
     <span style="color: rgb(255, 76, 0);">
      <strong>
       <span new="" style="line-height: 115%;" times="">
        What are the benefits of learning improvisation if you’re not an actor?
       </span>
      </strong>
     </span>
    </p>
    <p style="font-family: 16px 0px">
     <span style="color: rgb(255, 76, 0);">
      <strong>
       <span style="color: rgb(255, 76, 0); line-height: 115%; font-family: SimSun; font-size: 16px;">
        非职演员学习即兴表演的益处？
       </span>
      </strong>
     </span>
    </p>
    <p style="font-family: 16px 0px">
     <strong>
      <span new="" style=" line-height: 115% " times="">
      </span>
     </strong>
    </p>
    <p style="font-family: 0px 0px 13px">
     <span new="" style=" line-height: 115% " times="">
      Improvisation
     </span>
     <span new="" style=" line-height: 115% " times="">
      teaches p
     </span>
     <span new="" style=" line-height: 115% " times="">
      eople to take risks, to be willing to make mistakes and still keep trying, not because there’s no fear, but by learning to walk through the fear in a fun, safe, enjoyable, non-threatening environment with a group of people who simply meet to play games together.
     </span>
    </p>
    <p style="font-family: 0px 0px 13px">
     <span style=";line-height: 115%;font-family: SimSun;font-size: 16px">
      即兴表演让我们学会承担风险，勇于犯错并不断尝试。表演的过程中免不了“即兴”的压力和随之而来的害怕，但也正是即兴表演使我们能够以游戏的方式，在有趣、安全、没有威胁感的环境中去直面自己内心的害怕，与它共处。
     </span>
    </p>
    <p style="font-family: 0px 0px 13px">
     <span style=";line-height: 115%;font-family: SimSun;font-size: 16px">
     </span>
    </p>
    <p>
     <br/>
    </p>
    <p style="font-family: 0px 0px 13px">
     <span new="" style=" line-height: 115% " times="">
      Improv teaches us teamwork, creativity, trust and honesty with ourselves and others. Improv seriously increases our ability to communicate with other people and to actively listen to them. Improv increases our overall appreciation for life because it allows us to better understand how we humans act, react and experience an endless variety of life situations; to explore ourselves.
     </span>
    </p>
    <p style="font-family: 0px 0px 13px">
     <span style=";line-height: 115%;font-family: SimSun;font-size: 16px">
      即兴表演教会我们团队合作、创造力、信任、对自己及对他人诚实；能够增强我们倾听他人，与他人沟通的能力。通过即兴表演，我们更好地认识人们在日常生活中行动、反应的模式，我们创造出无数的生活场景，并在自己的即兴应对中发现自己，了解自己。
     </span>
    </p>
