    即兴舞蹈 | 接触即兴潜动谱工作坊Underscore  Workshop

    <p>
     <img data-ratio="0.6188557614826753" data-s="300,640" data-src="/assets/images/9d0ff9fd43.jpeg" data-type="jpeg" data-w="1241" src="/assets/images/9d0ff9fd43.jpeg" style="width: 100%; height: auto;"/>

     <br/>

    <p>
     <img data-ratio="0.6278026905829597" data-s="300,640" data-src="/assets/images/b9f23743cf.jpeg" data-type="jpeg" data-w="1115" src="/assets/images/b9f23743cf.jpeg" style="width: 100%; height: auto;"/>

    <p>
     <span style="font-size: 14px;">
      Underscore(潜动谱)是把接触即兴扩至更阔的即兴舞蹈练习层面，它让人们更自在地在空间中跳舞——独舞或者和他人一起跳；在即兴中整合运动知觉的与组合。它容纳着所有范围的能量与肢体表达，包含 多样的形式和变化的状态。这种练习既是熟悉的，又是不可预测的。
     </span>
    </p>
    <p>
     <span style="font-size: 14px;">
      这个练习一般持续几个小时，通过一系列充满活力的状态来向前推进，既包括长时间的细微、私人、内在的活动，也包括较高的能量状态和与他人互动的舞蹈。
     </span>
    </p>
    <p>
     <span style="font-size: 14px;">
      Underscore
      <span style="font-size: 14px; line-height: 24.8889px;">
       (潜动谱)
      </span>
      是由接触即兴教母Nancy Stark Smith所发展出来的长篇即兴舞蹈结构，自1990年以来它一直在不断发展，并在全球各地进行。
     </span>
     <img data-ratio="0.8757961783439491" data-s="300,640" data-src="/assets/images/e6b1c629b0.jpeg" data-type="jpeg" data-w="628" src="/assets/images/e6b1c629b0.jpeg" style="width: 100%; height: auto;"/>
    </p>
    <p style="text-align: right;">
     <span style="font-size: 14px; color: rgb(136, 136, 136);">
      香港 丸仔 提供
     </span>
    </p>
    <p>
     <span style="font-size: 14px;">
      Underscore有二十多个阶段，每个都有其名称与图形象征，为舞者创造了一张总体的地图。在这个框架之内，舞者可以自由地创造他们自己的动作、动态、关系——包括与自己、与他人、与团体、与音乐、与环境的关系。每个underscore都是独一无二的，提供着丰富和具有启发性的经验，这种经验关于人，也关于舞蹈即兴中的艺术现象。
     </span>
    </p>
    <p>
     <span style="font-size: 14px;">
      参加潜动谱，一般来说需要有一些即兴舞蹈经验及须曾参加一个小时多的潜动谱讲解(talk through)。需要知道的是，这是一个团体的旅程，所以希望你能够承诺全程参加活动，这样它就会比舞酱更有结构性。
     </span>
    </p>
    <p>
     <img data-ratio="1.6976127320954908" data-s="300,640" data-src="/assets/images/c6ae903ef3.jpeg" data-type="jpeg" data-w="754" src="/assets/images/c6ae903ef3.jpeg" style="width: 100%; height: auto;"/>
    </p>
    <p style="text-align: right;">
     <span style="font-size: 14px; color: rgb(136, 136, 136);">
      香港 丸仔 提供
     </span>
    </p>
    <p>
     <span style="font-size: 14px;">
      The Underscore is a long-form dance improvisation structure developed by Nancy Stark Smith. It has been evolving since 1990 and is practiced all over the globe.
     </span>
    </p>
    <p>
     <span style="font-size: 14px;">
      The Underscore is a vehicle for incorporating Contact Improvisation into a broader arena of improvisational dance practice; for developing greater ease dancing in spherical space—alone and with others; and for integrating kinesthetic and compositional concerns while improvising. It allows for a full spectrum of energetic and physical expressions, embodying a range of forms and changing states. Its practice is familiar yet unpredictable.
     </span>
    </p>
    <p>
     <img data-ratio="0.75" data-s="300,640" data-src="/assets/images/808bacc445.jpeg" data-type="jpeg" data-w="1280" src="/assets/images/808bacc445.jpeg" style="width: 100%; height: auto;"/>
    </p>
    <p style="text-align: right;">
     <span style="font-size: 14px; color: rgb(136, 136, 136);">
      北京Candy 提供
     </span>
    </p>
    <p>
     <span style="font-size: 14px;">
      The practice—usually 3 to 4 hours in length—progresses through a broad range of dynamic states, including long periods of very small, private, and quiet internal activity and other times of higher energy and interactive dancing.
     </span>
    </p>
    <p>
     <span style="font-size: 14px;">
      There are 20+ phases of the score—each with a name and a graphic symbol—which create a general map for the dancers. Within that frame, dancers are free to create their own movements, dynamics, and relationships—with themselves, each other, the group, the music, and the environment. Each Underscore is unique, providing rich and often inspiring experiences of the human and artistic phenomena of dance improvisation.
     </span>
    </p>
    <p>
     <span style="font-size: 14px;">
      To participate in an Underscore, one should have some experience of Contact Improvisation and attend a talk-through of the Underscore, which often takes about an hour.
     </span>
    </p>

    </p>
