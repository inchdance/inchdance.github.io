    <p style="line-height: normal;">
     <span style="font-size: 12px;">
      应奇轩, 以 DJ QuestionMark 在音乐界闻名, 他从高中开始玩音乐。通过他在大学的表演课, 他学习了剧场配乐, 也引此开启了他的音乐生涯。后来, 他开始为商业、电影甚至时装秀创作音乐。他很擅长创作现代和浪漫的音乐, 每次都努力提高他的音乐质量。曾应邀参加 i•dance 台北, 结合音乐与现代舞和表演，与当地与国外的音乐家和舞蹈家合作, 包括钢琴家麦克维加斯, 即兴大师南希史塔克史密斯, 和 古舞团。在 2015年, 他制作了室内乐乐团 SEMIFUSA 的同名专辑, 并后来被提名金曲奖最佳制作人。
     </span>
    </p>
    <p style="text-align: inherit; line-height: normal;">
     <span style="font-size: 12px;">
      <span style="font-size: 14px;">
       He started DJing from high school. Through an acting class he took in NTU, he was introduced to theatre scoring, which at the end inspired him to start a career in the music industry. Later, he started to create music for commercial, movies and even fashion shows. Very good at composing modern and romantic music, he tries to improve the quality of his music every time. Invited as an artist to attend i•dance Taipei, he combined music with modern dance and performed with both foreign and local musicians and dancers, including pianist Mike Vargas, contact improvisation master Nancy Stark Smith, and Ku &amp; Dancers. In 2015, he produced chamber orchestra SEMIFUSA’s self-titled album, and was later nominated for Producer of the Year at 27th Golden
