     <span style="font-size: 17px;">
      <strong>
       <span style="background-color: rgb(255, 218, 81);color: rgb(136, 136, 136);">
        潜动谱练习 Underscore practice
       </span>
      </strong>
     </span>
    </p>
    <p arial="arial" gb="gb" helvetica="helvetica" hiragino="hiragino" justify="justify" microsoft="microsoft" neue="neue" none="none" normal="normal" pingfang="pingfang" px="px" rgb="rgb" sans="sans" sans-serif="sans-serif" sc="sc" style='max-width: 100%;box-sizing: border-box;overflow-wrap: break-word;min-height: 1em;font-family: -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Helvetica Neue", "PingFang SC", "Hiragino Sans GB", "Microsoft YaHei UI", "Microsoft YaHei", Arial, sans-serif;font-variant-numeric: normal;font-variant-east-asian: normal;white-space: normal;background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);text-align: right;letter-spacing: 0.48px;' ui="ui" yahei="yahei">
     <span style="max-width: 100%;box-sizing: border-box;overflow-wrap: break-word;background-color: transparent;color: rgb(136, 136, 136);font-family: Calibri;font-size: 12px;font-variant-numeric: normal;font-variant-east-asian: normal;letter-spacing: normal;text-align: justify;">
      <span style="max-width: 100%;text-align: right;font-variant-numeric: normal;font-variant-east-asian: normal;background-color: transparent;">
       （wei中文翻译
      </span>
      ）
     </span>
    </p>
    <p arial="arial" gb="gb" helvetica="helvetica" hiragino="hiragino" justify="justify" microsoft="microsoft" neue="neue" none="none" normal="normal" pingfang="pingfang" px="px" rgb="rgb" sans="sans" sans-serif="sans-serif" sc="sc" style='max-width: 100%;box-sizing: border-box;overflow-wrap: break-word;min-height: 1em;font-family: -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Helvetica Neue", "PingFang SC", "Hiragino Sans GB", "Microsoft YaHei UI", "Microsoft YaHei", Arial, sans-serif;font-variant-numeric: normal;font-variant-east-asian: normal;white-space: normal;background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);letter-spacing: 0.48px;' ui="ui" yahei="yahei">
     <strong>
      <span style="max-width: 100%;box-sizing: border-box;overflow-wrap: break-word;color: rgb(136, 136, 136);font-family: Calibri;font-size: 12px;">
       Underscore（潜动谱）是一个长篇舞蹈结构即兴练习，由接触即兴之母 Nancy Starks Smith （1952-2020）从1900年不断发展至今。
      </span>
     </strong>
     <span style="max-width: 100%;box-sizing: border-box;overflow-wrap: break-word;color: rgb(136, 136, 136);font-family: Calibri;font-size: 12px;">
      把接触即兴扩至更阔的即兴舞蹈练习层面，它让人们更自在地在空间中跳舞——独舞或者和他人一起跳；在即兴中整合运动知觉的与组合。它容纳着所有范围的能量与肢体表达，包含 多样的形式和变化的状态。这种练习既是熟悉的，又是不可预测的。
     </span>
    </p>
    <p arial="arial" gb="gb" helvetica="helvetica" hiragino="hiragino" justify="justify" microsoft="microsoft" neue="neue" none="none" normal="normal" pingfang="pingfang" px="px" rgb="rgb" sans="sans" sans-serif="sans-serif" sc="sc" style='max-width: 100%;box-sizing: border-box;overflow-wrap: break-word;min-height: 1em;font-family: -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Helvetica Neue", "PingFang SC", "Hiragino Sans GB", "Microsoft YaHei UI", "Microsoft YaHei", Arial, sans-serif;font-variant-numeric: normal;font-variant-east-asian: normal;white-space: normal;background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);letter-spacing: 0.48px;' ui="ui" yahei="yahei">
     <span style="max-width: 100%;box-sizing: border-box;overflow-wrap: break-word;color: rgb(136, 136, 136);font-size: 12px;">
      这个练习一般持续几个小时，通过一系列充满活力的状态来向前推进，既包括长时间的细微、私人、内在的活动，也包括较高的能量状态和与他人互动的舞蹈。
     </span>
    </p>
    <p arial="arial" gb="gb" helvetica="helvetica" hiragino="hiragino" justify="justify" microsoft="microsoft" neue="neue" none="none" normal="normal" pingfang="pingfang" px="px" rgb="rgb" sans="sans" sans-serif="sans-serif" sc="sc" style='max-width: 100%;box-sizing: border-box;overflow-wrap: break-word;min-height: 1em;font-family: -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Helvetica Neue", "PingFang SC", "Hiragino Sans GB", "Microsoft YaHei UI", "Microsoft YaHei", Arial, sans-serif;font-variant-numeric: normal;font-variant-east-asian: normal;white-space: normal;background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);letter-spacing: 0.48px;' ui="ui" yahei="yahei">
     <strong>
      <span style="max-width: 100%;box-sizing: border-box;overflow-wrap: break-word;color: rgb(136, 136, 136);font-family: Calibri;font-size: 12px;">
       潜动谱有二十多个阶段的标记，每一个都有名字和图形符号 — 这提供给了舞者一幅全局图。
      </span>
     </strong>
     <span style="max-width: 100%;box-sizing: border-box;overflow-wrap: break-word;color: rgb(136, 136, 136);font-family: Calibri;font-size: 12px;">
      在这个框架内，舞者自由地创造自己的舞动，动力，和关系 — 有和自己，和他人，和群组，和音乐，和环境的关系。每次潜动谱都是独一无二的，给参与者们提供了即兴舞动中丰富而鼓舞人心的人文体验和艺术效应。
     </span>
    </p>
    <p arial="arial" gb="gb" helvetica="helvetica" hiragino="hiragino" justify="justify" microsoft="microsoft" neue="neue" none="none" normal="normal" pingfang="pingfang" px="px" rgb="rgb" sans="sans" sans-serif="sans-serif" sc="sc" style='max-width: 100%;box-sizing: border-box;overflow-wrap: break-word;min-height: 1em;font-family: -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Helvetica Neue", "PingFang SC", "Hiragino Sans GB", "Microsoft YaHei UI", "Microsoft YaHei", Arial, sans-serif;font-variant-numeric: normal;font-variant-east-asian: normal;white-space: normal;background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);letter-spacing: 0.48px;' ui="ui" yahei="yahei">
     <span style="max-width: 100%;box-sizing: border-box;overflow-wrap: break-word;color: rgb(136, 136, 136);font-family: Calibri;font-size: 12px;">
      <br/>
     </span>
    </p>
    <p arial="arial" gb="gb" helvetica="helvetica" hiragino="hiragino" justify="justify" microsoft="microsoft" neue="neue" none="none" normal="normal" pingfang="pingfang" px="px" rgb="rgb" sans="sans" sans-serif="sans-serif" sc="sc" style='max-width: 100%;box-sizing: border-box;overflow-wrap: break-word;min-height: 1em;font-family: -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Helvetica Neue", "PingFang SC", "Hiragino Sans GB", "Microsoft YaHei UI", "Microsoft YaHei", Arial, sans-serif;font-variant-numeric: normal;font-variant-east-asian: normal;white-space: normal;background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);letter-spacing: 0.48px;' ui="ui" yahei="yahei">
     <strong>
      <span style="max-width: 100%;box-sizing: border-box;overflow-wrap: break-word;color: rgb(136, 136, 136);font-family: Calibri;font-size: 12px;">
       The Underscore is a long-form dance improvisation structure developed by Nancy Stark Smith
       <strong style='font-family: -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Helvetica Neue", "PingFang SC", "Hiragino Sans GB", "Microsoft YaHei UI", "Microsoft YaHei", Arial, sans-serif;letter-spacing: 0.48px;white-space: normal;'>
        （1952-2020）
       </strong>
       . It has been evolving since 1990 and is practiced all over the globe.
      </span>
     </strong>
    </p>
    <p arial="arial" gb="gb" helvetica="helvetica" hiragino="hiragino" justify="justify" microsoft="microsoft" neue="neue" none="none" normal="normal" pingfang="pingfang" px="px" rgb="rgb" sans="sans" sans-serif="sans-serif" sc="sc" style='max-width: 100%;box-sizing: border-box;overflow-wrap: break-word;min-height: 1em;font-family: -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Helvetica Neue", "PingFang SC", "Hiragino Sans GB", "Microsoft YaHei UI", "Microsoft YaHei", Arial, sans-serif;font-variant-numeric: normal;font-variant-east-asian: normal;white-space: normal;background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);letter-spacing: 0.48px;' ui="ui" yahei="yahei">
     <span style="max-width: 100%;box-sizing: border-box;overflow-wrap: break-word;color: rgb(136, 136, 136);font-family: Calibri;font-size: 14px;">
      The Underscore is a vehicle for incorporating Contact Improvisation into a broader arena of improvisational dance practice; for developing greater ease dancing in spherical space—alone and with others; and for integrating kinesthetic and compositional concerns while improvising. It allows for a full spectrum of energetic and physical expressions, embodying a range of forms and changing states. Its practice is familiar yet unpredictable.
     </span>
    </p>
    <p arial="arial" gb="gb" helvetica="helvetica" hiragino="hiragino" justify="justify" microsoft="microsoft" neue="neue" none="none" normal="normal" pingfang="pingfang" px="px" rgb="rgb" sans="sans" sans-serif="sans-serif" sc="sc" style='max-width: 100%;box-sizing: border-box;overflow-wrap: break-word;min-height: 1em;font-family: -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Helvetica Neue", "PingFang SC", "Hiragino Sans GB", "Microsoft YaHei UI", "Microsoft YaHei", Arial, sans-serif;font-variant-numeric: normal;font-variant-east-asian: normal;white-space: normal;background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);letter-spacing: 0.48px;' ui="ui" yahei="yahei">
     <span style="max-width: 100%;box-sizing: border-box;overflow-wrap: break-word;background-color: transparent;color: rgb(136, 136, 136);font-family: Calibri;font-size: 14px;font-variant-numeric: normal;font-variant-east-asian: normal;letter-spacing: normal;">
      The practice—usually 3 to 4 hours in length—progresses through a broad range of dynamic states, including long periods of very small, private, and quiet internal activity and other times of higher energy and interactive dancing.
     </span>
    </p>
    <p arial="arial" gb="gb" helvetica="helvetica" hiragino="hiragino" justify="justify" microsoft="microsoft" neue="neue" none="none" normal="normal" pingfang="pingfang" px="px" rgb="rgb" sans="sans" sans-serif="sans-serif" sc="sc" style='max-width: 100%;box-sizing: border-box;overflow-wrap: break-word;min-height: 1em;font-family: -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Helvetica Neue", "PingFang SC", "Hiragino Sans GB", "Microsoft YaHei UI", "Microsoft YaHei", Arial, sans-serif;font-variant-numeric: normal;font-variant-east-asian: normal;white-space: normal;background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);letter-spacing: 0.48px;' ui="ui" yahei="yahei">
     <strong>
      <span style="max-width: 100%;box-sizing: border-box;overflow-wrap: break-word;background-color: transparent;color: rgb(136, 136, 136);font-family: Calibri;font-size: 14px;font-variant-numeric: normal;font-variant-east-asian: normal;letter-spacing: normal;">
       There are 20+ phases of the score—each with a name and a graphic symbol—which create a general map for the dancers.
      </span>
     </strong>
     <span style="max-width: 100%;box-sizing: border-box;overflow-wrap: break-word;background-color: transparent;color: rgb(136, 136, 136);font-family: Calibri;font-size: 14px;font-variant-numeric: normal;font-variant-east-asian: normal;letter-spacing: normal;">
      Within that frame, dancers are free to create their own movements, dynamics, and relationships—with themselves, each other, the group, the music, and the environment. Each Underscore is unique, providing rich and often inspiring experiences of the human and artistic phenomena of dance improvisation.
      <br style="max-width: 100%;box-sizing: border-box;overflow-wrap: break-word;"/>
     </span>
    </p>
    <p arial="arial" gb="gb" helvetica="helvetica" hiragino="hiragino" justify="justify" microsoft="microsoft" neue="neue" none="none" normal="normal" pingfang="pingfang" px="px" rgb="rgb" sans="sans" sans-serif="sans-serif" sc="sc" style='max-width: 100%;box-sizing: border-box;overflow-wrap: break-word;min-height: 1em;font-family: -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Helvetica Neue", "PingFang SC", "Hiragino Sans GB", "Microsoft YaHei UI", "Microsoft YaHei", Arial, sans-serif;font-variant-numeric: normal;font-variant-east-asian: normal;white-space: normal;background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);letter-spacing: 0.48px;' ui="ui" yahei="yahei">
