    <p>
     <span style="color: rgb(0, 128, 0);font-size: 14px;display: inline !important;float: none;background-color: transparent;">
      Underscore (潜动谱)基于每个人独特的身体，背景，经历以及敏感度，回归每个人不同的个性，探索多元化的身心美学，在一个美好的结构内，完成内在探索，个性交流和共同创造。在一个框架指导下建立人与人的关系，并能享受极富乐趣的天马行空的身体对话。
     </span>
    </p>
    <p>
     <span style="color: rgb(0, 128, 0);font-size: 14px;display: inline !important;float: none;background-color: transparent;">
      虽然是一起跳舞，但是它并不讲究舞步动作的正确与否，反而强调从内在去经验动作，允许舞蹈自己发生。人们在其中阅读时间空间和关系，洞察自己及他人的身体，探索、发展和见证各种可能性。
     </span>

    <p style="text-align: left;">
     <span style="color: rgb(0, 0, 0);font-size: 12px;background-color: transparent;">
      Underscore(中文译为：潜动谱)是由接触即兴教母Nancy Stark Smith所发展出来的长篇即兴舞蹈结构，自1990年以来它一直在不断发展，并在全球各地进行。
     </span>
    </p>
    <p style="text-align: left;">
     <br/>
    </p>
    <p style="text-align: center;">
     <span style="background-color: transparent;color: rgb(0, 0, 0);">

     </span>
    </p>
    <p style="text-align: center;">
     <span style="font-size: 12px;">
      (图片来自网络）
     </span>
     <span style="background-color: transparent;color: rgb(0, 0, 0);">
      <br/>
     </span>
    </p>
    <p style="text-align: left;">
     <span style="font: 400 12px/18.33px Calibri;margin: 0px;padding: 0px;text-align: left;color: rgb(0, 0, 0);text-transform: none;text-indent: 0px;letter-spacing: normal;text-decoration: none;word-spacing: 0px;display: inline !important;word-wrap: break-word;max-width: 100%;orphans: 2;font-size-adjust: none;font-stretch: normal;float: none;-webkit-text-stroke-width: 0px;background-color: transparent;">
      wei翻译from http://nancystarksmith.com/underscore/ ：
     </span>
    </p>
    <p style="text-align: left;">
     <span style="font: 400 12px/18.33px Calibri;margin: 0px;padding: 0px;text-align: left;color: rgb(0, 0, 0);text-transform: none;text-indent: 0px;letter-spacing: normal;text-decoration: none;word-spacing: 0px;display: inline !important;word-wrap: break-word;max-width: 100%;orphans: 2;font-size-adjust: none;font-stretch: normal;float: none;-webkit-text-stroke-width: 0px;background-color: transparent;">
      Underscore is a vehicle forincorporating Contact Improvisation into a broader arena of improvisationaldance practice; for developing greater ease dancing in spherical space—aloneand with others; and for integrating kinesthetic and compositional concernswhile improvising. It allows for a full spectrum of energetic and physicalexpressions, embodying a range of forms and changing states. Its practice isfamiliar yet unpredictable.
     </span>
    </p>
    <p style="text-align: left;">
     <span style="font: 400 12px/18.33px Calibri;margin: 0px;padding: 0px;text-align: left;color: rgb(0, 0, 0);text-transform: none;text-indent: 0px;letter-spacing: normal;text-decoration: none;word-spacing: 0px;display: inline !important;word-wrap: break-word;max-width: 100%;orphans: 2;font-size-adjust: none;font-stretch: normal;float: none;-webkit-text-stroke-width: 0px;background-color: transparent;">
      Underscore（潜动谱）把接触即兴扩至更阔的即兴舞
     </span>
    </p>
    <p style="text-align: left;">
     <span style="font: 400 12px/18.33px Calibri;margin: 0px;padding: 0px;text-align: left;color: rgb(0, 0, 0);text-transform: none;text-indent: 0px;letter-spacing: normal;text-decoration: none;word-spacing: 0px;display: inline !important;word-wrap: break-word;max-width: 100%;orphans: 2;font-size-adjust: none;font-stretch: normal;float: none;-webkit-text-stroke-width: 0px;background-color: transparent;">
      蹈练习层面，它让人们更自在地在空间中跳舞——独舞或者和他人一起跳；在即兴中整合运动知觉的与组合。它容纳着所有范围的能量与肢体表达，包含 多样的形式和变化的状态。这种练习既是熟悉的，又是不可预测的。
     </span>
    </p>

     <span style="font: 400 12px/18.33px Calibri;margin: 0px;padding: 0px;text-align: left;color: rgb(0, 0, 0);text-transform: none;text-indent: 0px;letter-spacing: normal;text-decoration: none;word-spacing: 0px;display: inline !important;word-wrap: break-word;max-width: 100%;orphans: 2;font-size-adjust: none;font-stretch: normal;float: none;-webkit-text-stroke-width: 0px;background-color: transparent;">
      The practice—usually 3 to 4 hours in length—progresses through a broad range ofdynamic states, including long periods of very small, private, and quietinternal activity and other times of higher energy and interactive dancing.
     </span>
    </p>
    <p style="text-align: left;">
     <span style="font-size: 12px;">
      这个练习一般持续几个小时，通过一系列充满活力的状态来向前推进，既包括长时间的细微、私人、内在的活动，也包括较高的能量状态和与他人互动的舞蹈。
     </span>
    </p>

    <p style="text-align: left;">
     <span style="font: 400 12px/18.33px Calibri;margin: 0px;padding: 0px;text-align: left;color: rgb(0, 0, 0);text-transform: none;text-indent: 0px;letter-spacing: normal;text-decoration: none;word-spacing: 0px;display: inline !important;word-wrap: break-word;max-width: 100%;orphans: 2;font-size-adjust: none;font-stretch: normal;float: none;-webkit-text-stroke-width: 0px;background-color: transparent;">
      There are 20+ phases of the score—each witha name and a graphic symbol—which create a general map for the dancers. Withinthat frame, dancers are free to create their own movements, dynamics, andrelationships—with themselves, each other, the group, the music, and theenvironment. Each Underscore is unique, providing rich and often inspiringexperiences of the human and artistic phenomena of dance improvisation.
     </span>
    </p>
    <p style="text-align: left;">
     <span style="font: 400 12px/18.33px Calibri;margin: 0px;padding: 0px;text-align: left;color: rgb(0, 0, 0);text-transform: none;text-indent: 0px;letter-spacing: normal;text-decoration: none;word-spacing: 0px;display: inline !important;word-wrap: break-word;max-width: 100%;orphans: 2;font-size-adjust: none;font-stretch: normal;float: none;-webkit-text-stroke-width: 0px;background-color: transparent;">
      潜动谱有二十多个阶段的标记，每一个都有名字和图形符号 — 这提供给了舞者一幅全局图。在这个框架内，舞者自由地创造自己的舞动，动力，和关系 — 有和自己，和他人，和群组，和音乐，和环境的关系。每次潜动谱都是独一无二的，给参与者们提供了即兴舞动中丰富而鼓舞人心的人文体验和艺术效应。
     </span>
    </p>
