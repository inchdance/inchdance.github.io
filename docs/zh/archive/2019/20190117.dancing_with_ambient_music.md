    <p>
     <span style="color: rgb(136, 136, 136);font-size: 15px;">
      Empress CC！是一位中国出生的，伦敦培育的音乐人和DJ。从小受过古典钢琴训练钢琴，在威斯敏斯特音频制作系毕业，于2014年发行了她的第一张个人专辑。自回到上海后，她潜入了DJ世界。与她的精神追求相承，她今年做了较多的环境音乐。相信无（传统）节拍音乐的力量，她以音乐的质感触发的情感流动和连接是为向导。来Empresss CC!的声音世界听歌，在醒着的时候造梦。
     </span>
    </p>
    <p>
     <br/>
    </p>
    <p>
     <span style="color: rgb(136, 136, 136);font-size: 15px;">
      Empress CC! is a Chinese born and musically London raised musician and DJ. She was classically trained pianist and studied MA Audio Production in Westminster university, accordingly released her first personal album in 2014. Since coming back to Shanghai, she has dived into the DJ world . In connection with her spiritual persuit, she has made more ambient music this year. Believing in the power of music with non ( traditional ) beats, the textures in music that triggers emotions to flow and to connect are her guide. Emerge in the sound world of Empress CC! and make dreamscape while awake.
     </span>
