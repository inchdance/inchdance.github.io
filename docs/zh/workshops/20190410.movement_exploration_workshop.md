     <span style="color: rgb(0, 0, 0);font-size: 15px;">
      <span lang="ZH-CN" style="margin: 0px;color: rgb(0, 0, 0);font-family: 宋体;">
       本工作坊从现代舞蹈的基本概念和运动研究中提出了对运动的探索：时间，空间和能量。
      </span>
      <span lang="ZH-CN" style="margin: 0px;color: rgb(0, 0, 0);font-family: Roboto;">
      </span>
      <span lang="ZH-CN" style="margin: 0px;color: rgb(0, 0, 0);font-family: 宋体;">
       中国传统技术的缓慢和即兴表演的速度将被用来探索不同运动持续的时间和密度。运动中呼吸的方式也有助于意识到运动中不同姿态的各种能量。
      </span>
      <span lang="ZH-CN" style="margin: 0px;color: rgb(0, 0, 0);font-family: Roboto;">
      </span>
      <span lang="ZH-CN" style="margin: 0px;color: rgb(0, 0, 0);font-family: 宋体;">
       工作坊将运用个人到二人和小组的渐进式练习方式，探索内在感知和集体振动之间的关系。
      </span>
     </span>
    </p>

      <br/>
     </span>

    <p style="margin: 0px;">
     <span style="margin: 0px;color: rgb(0, 0, 0);font-family: Microsoft YaHei;font-size: 15px;">
      This
class propose a exploration of movement from basic concepts of contemporary
dance and research in motion: time, space and energy. Slowness from traditional
Chinese techniques and speed from improvisation performance are used to explore
different duration and density of movement. The way of breathing in motion help
also to become aware of the various energy of gesture. At last, progressive
teaching learning from individual to duo and group exercises help to explore
the relation between inner perception and collective vibration.
     </span>
