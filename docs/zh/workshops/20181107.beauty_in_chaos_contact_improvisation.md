      Bartenieff Fundamentals consist of a set of concepts, principles, and exercises developed by a student and colleague of Rudolf Laban, Irmgard Bartenieff, in the 1950’s through 1970’s.  She applied Laban’s movement ideas to the physical and kinesiological functioning of the human body.  This body of work has been successfully used to enhance the physical functioning of professional athletes, dancers, actors, performers, as well as with people in all aspects of everyday life.
