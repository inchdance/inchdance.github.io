   </h1>
   <div class="rich_media_content js_underline_content" id="js_content" style=" ">
    <p style="text-align: center;">
     <strong>
      <span new="" style="color: black;" times="">
       <img data-ratio="0.75" data-s="300,640" data-type="jpeg" data-w="1280" src="/assets/images/4bb00ea5e2.jpg" style="width: 100%; max-width: 600px;"/>

    <p style="text-align: center;">
     <strong>
      <span new="" style="color: black;" times="">
       关于工作坊
      </span>
     </strong>
     <span new="" style="color: black;" times="">
      <br/>
     </span>
    </p>
    <p style="line-height: 27px; text-indent: 32px;">
     我们的身体就如同个小宇宙，里面蕴含著无限的能量。但是，生活中的压力，往往让这些能量变弱或是被隐藏。工作坊将从走路出发，透过觉察自己和他人的身体间的能量流动，将身体的小宇宙能量带回，让身体蓄积能量再度出发。
    </p>
    <p style="line-height: 27px;">
     <span style="font-family: Calibri;">
     </span>
     身体和身体间的小宇宙一旦连结，会爆发出更有趣的能量，而且个个能量不同，在探索自己的小宇宙之馀，我们也将和其他的身体小宇宙互相碰撞、对话。
    </p>
    <p style="line-height: 27px;">
     <span new="" style="color: black;" times="">
     </span>
    </p>
    <p style="text-align: center; line-height: 27px;">
     <strong>
      About the workshop:
     </strong>
    </p>
    <p style="text-align: center; line-height: 24px;">
     Our bodies are like small universes,which contain infinite energy. But the stresses in life tend to weaken or hidethese energies. The workshop will start with walking, by sensing the flow ofenergy between oneself and other people's bodies, bringing back the smallcosmic energy of the body, allowing the body to store up energy and startagain.
    </p>
    <p style="text-align: center; line-height: 24px;">
     <span style='color: black; font-family: "Helvetica","sans-serif"; font-size: 16px; mso-fareast-font-family: 新細明體; mso-fareast-theme-font: minor-fareast; mso-ansi-language: EN-US; mso-fareast-language: ZH-TW; mso-bidi-language: AR-SA;'>
      Let us release the small universe in the body and follow the dancers’ pace to feel the energy flow in your body.
     </span>

       is a multidisciplinary performing artist,educator and choreographer. While originally from Italy she has spent thelast 16 years living in Europe, India and China. At present she is basedin Beijing. Her formal training is in dance, classical music (flute and voice)and theatre.Her artistic path has been influenced by many different culturesand is deeply intertwined with her self-inquiry, spiritual practices andphilosophy. Her dance practice is mainly inspired by ContactImprovisation, Instant Composition Techniques, Experiential Anatomy,Body-Mind Centering, Body Work, Yoga, Meditation and Singing Practices.The main theme of her movement research is Improvisation. In 2011 she createdBeing Motion, a platform for sharing her artistic practices, research,creations and educational projects.

Some of the sculptures covered eyes with hands, as if peeping out the world; some walk with the"companion" side by side, as if a mutual support to each other after drunk. The sculptures have a kind of magic that temporarily draws people out of the chaos. This magic also inspires the dancers to communicate with these culptures by using their body.
