<p>
<span style="font-family: PingFangTC-Light;font-size: 13px;color: #000000;">The Dance to Save the Nation Theory is a bold and interesting idea.</span>
</p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: PingFangTC-Light;font-size: 13px;color: #000000;">Upon closer examination, it fits Gumin Shen's personality perfectly, carefree and light yet thoughtful.</span>

<p>
<span style="font-family: PingFangTC-Light;font-size: 13px;color: #000000;">Famous Taiwanese choreographer Yen-Fang Yu once described her mentor Gumin Shen with the words 'graceful and light.' It's spot on. Whether watching Gumin Shen's dance or listening to her speak, she always comes across as elegant and articulate. As an outside observer, the deeper I delve into her works and watch her dances, the more I am attracted to her inherent boldness and unruliness.</span>

<p>
<span style="font-family: PingFangTC-Light;font-size: 13px;color: #000000;">After graduating with a master's degree in dance from the University of Illinois, USA, Gumin Shen served as the head of the dance department, director of the graduate institute, and dean of the dance school at the National Taiwan University of Arts. She received the Zhongxing Art Award in 1997 and the Wu San Lien Art Award in 2009.</span>
</p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: PingFangTC-Light;font-size: 13px;color: #000000;">
<br/>
</span>
</p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: PingFangTC-Light;font-size: 13px;color: #000000;">Among the many labels society has given her, she is best known as the 'Mother of Asian Contact Improvisation.'</span>

<p>
<span style="font-family: PingFangTC-Light;font-size: 13px;color: #000000;">

<p>
<span style="font-family: PingFangTC-Light;font-size: 13px;color: #000000;">In 1993, Gumin Shen founded the 'Gumin Shen Dance Company' (now called 'Gu Dance Company'). While engaging in modern dance creation and performance, she actively promoted improvisational dance performance and 'contact improvisation</span>
<span style="color: #000000;font-family: PingFangTC-Light;font-size: 13px;">styles and concepts. Over the past 26 years, the company has traveled to many countries and cities around the world, including Taiwan, Hong Kong, the United States, the United Kingdom, Australia, and Europe, and has become<span style="text-shadow: #d7aba9 2px 2px 10px;">the only professional performing group in Taiwan using this form of performance art.</span>.</span>

<span style="font-family: PingFangTC-Light;font-size: 13px;color: #888888;">The core of a society is each individual person. Learning to dance is also about learning self-awareness, knowing what you are doing, a path filled with discipline and mindfulness.</span>

<span style="color: #000000;font-family: PingFangTC-Light;font-size: 13px;">What is the Shanghai Improvisation Festival? Many people ask this. I would answer them: This is a platform most focused on 'people'. It uses professional skills to absorb and assimilate everyone and everything. Here, you can use the most primitive methods and abilities to explore and develop the body's instincts, to establish a relationship with yourself, and to establish relationships with others.<span style="color: #000000;font-family: PingFangTC-Light;font-size: 13px;">The Shanghai Improvisation Festival has shown me more possibilities for improvisation and dance, as well as the positive impact it can have on society,” said Gu Ming Shen.</span>
