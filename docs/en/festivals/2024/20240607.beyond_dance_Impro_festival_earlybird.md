     <section style="font-size: 14px;line-height: 1.75;padding-right: 20px;padding-left: 20px;">
      <p style="text-wrap: wrap;">
       As an active choreographer and dancer, Ming-Shen Ku has settled her base in Taiwan(CN) since 1987. Ms. Ku’s works are influenced by many western and eastern dance styles, a merging development from her diverse backgrounds.
      </p>
      <p style="text-wrap: wrap;">
       <br/>
      </p>
      <p style="text-wrap: wrap;">
       Since 1992, Ms. Ku became deeply involved in Contact Improvisation and introduced it to Taiwan. She founded her dance company “Ku &amp; Dancers” in 1993 to present new works and promote the concept of improvisation. “Ku &amp; Dancers” has set their footprint in New York, Australia, Paris, London, the mainland of China, Korea, Malaysia, Indonesia and Japan.
      </p>
      <p style="text-wrap: wrap;">
       <br/>
      </p>
      <p style="text-wrap: wrap;">
       She has collaborated with many dance companies and events in Taiwan(CN), Hong Kong(CN), Japan, Australia, Austria as well as U.S.  Her solo performance has also been invited to tour in the US, London, Paris, Australia, Japan, Malaysia, the mainland of China and Hong Kong(CN). The review from New York Times has commented her work “simple and handsome”.
      </p>
      <p style="text-wrap: wrap;">
       <br/>
      </p>
      <p style="text-wrap: wrap;">
       Ms. Ku has also been invited as a guest artist to perform and teach in many universities and dance companies around the world. She received the Wu San Lien award, a lifetime achievement award in 2009, and the National Award(TW) for Arts in 2020.

      <p style="text-wrap: wrap;">
       Victor Choi-wo MA founded Y-Space in 1995 and he is the art director. He was in the first graduating class of the School of Dance of The Hong Kong Academy for Performing Arts. Upon graduation, he joined The Hong Kong Ballet, and resumed study at the HKAPA School of Drama in 1990. In 1996, he received the Asian Cultural Council Lee Hysan Foundation Fellowship to conduct research on dance and theatre in the USA.
      </p>
      <p style="text-wrap: wrap;">
       <br/>
      </p>
      <p style="text-wrap: wrap;">
       In 2002, he further received the Hong Kong Arts Development Council-University of Leeds-Chevening Scholarships for a Master’s degree programme in Performance Studies in the UK. As a choreographer with over 60 creations, he has also been a movement director for many local and international companies from Taiwan, China, South Korea, India, the USA, Australia, Germany, Norway, Denmark, Poland, Zimbabwe, etc.
      </p>
      <p style="text-wrap: wrap;">
       <br/>

He has received the Hong Kong Arts Development Awards 2016 on Artist of the Year (Dance). Starting from 2019, he organizes the “Organic Form and Transformation Training” one-year certificate course in Dance and Performing Arts, and serves as the Chief Designer and Instructor of the course.
