     <section style="letter-spacing: 1.5px;padding-right: 25px;padding-left: 25px;">
      <p style="white-space: normal;">
       Contact improvisation and improvisation are two seemingly similar but possibly very different topics. Contact improvisation is intended to be a dance behavior in which two people interact with each other by feeling the physical sensations transmitted by each other due to physical contact. This includes the cognition of one's own body, sensitivity to touch, and how to carry out the corresponding dance. The course will gradually
       <strong>
        guide students from the process of dancing as an individual to dancing with a partner from the fundamental to the deep
       </strong>
       .
      </p>
      <p style="white-space: normal;">
       <br/>
      </p>
      <p style="white-space: normal;">
       The dance triggered by the premise of body development and free dance is not only about the projection of personal satisfaction but
       <strong>
        also more about sharing the present and experience
       </strong>
       . Therefore, this course will also use game-like exercises to guide improvisation methods. All relevant key points include physical sensation, space, time, structure, and other sub-items. It is expected that students will
       <strong>
        deepen their understanding of themselves, care for their surroundings in practice, and practice sharing during the course
       </strong>
       .
      </p>

      <p style="white-space: normal;">
       Contact improvisation applies a completely equal body to all body parts. Whether it is a specific weight, center of gravity, or a piece of skin lightly touching, they all represent the passing of information. The advancement of technique contains, at the same time, the seemingly invisible refinement of sensitivity and the variety of choices in an instant.
       <strong>
       </strong>
       <strong>
        How to continue to enter unknown areas depends on the encounter and integration of all the links and elements.
       </strong>
       Furthermore,
       <strong>
        when improvisation becomes a performance, how compatible are the identities of the dancer and the performer?
       </strong>
       All current choices are fleeting and ever-changing decisions.
      </p>
      <p style="white-space: normal;">
       <br/>
      </p>
      <p style="white-space: normal;">
       The course of this advanced class will be arranged in two aspects: "Advanced Contact Improvisation Course" and "Performance Topic".
       <strong>
        'Advanced CI Course'
       </strong>
       includes how to find the most unexpected gaps between movement and stillness to challenge, and how to stabilize the center of gravity in a small area. The purpose of the
       <strong>
        'Performance Topic'
       </strong>
       is to improve the dancers' comprehensive consideration for the present situation and how to respond to the audience's gaze, etc.
